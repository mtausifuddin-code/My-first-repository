About Dr. Afraaz

Dr. Mohammad Afraaz (PT) is a dedicated and patient-centered physiotherapist specializing in Musculoskeletal, Neurological, and Sports Rehabilitation. He holds a Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) from Hitech Medical College and Hospital and serves as Consultant Physiotherapist at Prayatna Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation.

Specialized training includes Kinesio IASTM, Dry Needling, Myokinetic Chain Release, Neural Tissue Mobilisation, Craniosacral Therapy, Muscle Energy Technique, Postural & Ergonomic Assessment, Strength & Conditioning, Kinesio & Sports Taping, plus CPR/AED/First Aid certification.
